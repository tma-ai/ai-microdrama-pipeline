## The setting: the Vance Holdings boardroom
The top-floor boardroom of Vance Holdings, a shipping company tower on the harbour front. Night, a storm outside. Tonight is a private foreclosure signing: Marcus is taking over Pier Nine, the last asset of the Cross family's shipping firm.

**What you see:**
- **The room:** long and rectangular. A dark walnut conference table runs down the middle with 10 black leather chairs, and three warm brass pendant lamps hang over the table.
- **NORTH wall (behind Marcus):** floor-to-ceiling glass. Behind it is the harbour at night in the rain, with the lights of Pier Nine and its cranes. A rusted, battered cargo ship sits at the pier under bright work lights, with crews around it.
- **SOUTH wall (the far end):** tall double walnut doors with brass handles, and a security guard in a dark suit beside them. Everyone enters this way.
- **EAST wall (the long wall Elena faces across the table):** dark wood panelling with a row of lit glass cases holding scale models of ships. Each has a small brass plaque, and each is a ship Vance Holdings took over. One case at the end is empty, with its light still on.
- **WEST wall:** matching wood panelling with a long sideboard and a crystal decanter.
- **On the table at Marcus's place:** the deed of foreclosure, a fountain pen, the company seal stamp, his phone, and a black-ribboned memorial card from the Cross funeral. It's from the estate file and shows a photo of Julian and Sophia.
- **Light:** warm pendant lamps, the cold blue city and harbour light through the glass, and lightning flashes. Every light has a source.

## The episode (60 s, 6 scenes, 3 angles each; Antigravity's lines unchanged)
1. **HOOK (0–10 s).** ECU: Marcus's fountain pen signs the deed, then his hand turns the memorial card face-down. Wide: Marcus at the head, Elena halfway down the side, the rain-lashed glass behind them and the battered ship at the pier. Marcus stamps the seal: *"Pier Nine is officially foreclosed…"* Lightning, then a hard knock at the double doors.
2. **THE DEAD MAN (10–20 s).** The doors swing open; the guard turns. Julian walks in, coat soaked with rain, holding a black folder. Elena: *"The public gallery is closed. State your name and business."* He doesn't answer. He walks up the table.
3. **HUMILIATION (20–30 s).** Elena's eyes drop to the memorial card on the table, then rise to Julian's face; she recognises him. Marcus, colour draining: *"You drowned… you don't have standing in this room."* The guard grabs Julian's shoulder and pulls him back. Julian doesn't blink.
4. **TURN (30–40 s).** Julian: *"I didn't drown, Marcus. I raised the Aethelgard… and I brought her manifests."* His eyes go to the window, and Marcus turns: the battered ship at the pier. Marcus swallows. Julian drops the folder in front of Elena.
5. **FACE-SLAP (40–50 s).** Elena breaks the seal and reads: *"This isn't an appeal… Vance Holdings is legally frozen."* She stamps the order with her court seal and raises her hand, and the guard lets go of Julian. Marcus slams his fist on the table.
6. **CLIFFHANGER (50–60 s).** Julian turns the memorial card face-up in front of Marcus and leans to his ear: *"Sophia's car had Vance Holdings grease on the brake line. Tonight, Marcus, you pay every cent."* He walks out through the doors. Marcus's phone lights red and buzzes on the table. Cut before he answers: **who is calling?**
